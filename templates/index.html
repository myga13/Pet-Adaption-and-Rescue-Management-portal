Pet Care

Many families want to adopt but struggle to find trusted sources, clear information, and support after bringing a pet home.

Pet lovers need a simple way to discover verified pets, understand their needs, and feel confident that they’re making the right choice for their family.

What We Do

We provide a secure, NGO-moderated platform for reporting lost and found animals across species — from dogs and cats to farm animals and birds. Our verification process reduces fraud and helps reunify animals with their families quickly.

🛡️ NGO-verified reports to reduce false claims and improve trust
🔗 Support for all animal types including farm and working animals
❤️ Clear handover protocols to protect both animals and claimants
