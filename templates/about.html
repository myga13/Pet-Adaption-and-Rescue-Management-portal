About PawCare

PawCare was created with one simple belief: every animal deserves safety, love, and a place to call home. We bring together pet parents, NGOs, shelters, and animal lovers on a single, trusted platform.

From verified adoptions to NGO-approved lost & found reporting, our mission is to make animal care more transparent, humane, and community-driven.

“Technology with compassion — that’s how we help pets find their forever homes and families take better care of them.”

Our Mission

We aim to build India’s most trusted pet care and adoption ecosystem by:

  • Connecting real families with verified pets in need of homes.
  • Helping NGOs and shelters manage cases, reports, and adoptions efficiently.
  • Using data and digital tools to reduce fraud in lost & found reporting.
  • Spreading awareness about responsible pet parenting and animal welfare.
NGO-verified Pan-India support Community first

Who We Work With

PawCare is built for everyone who cares about animals:

  • Pet owners looking to adopt, rehome, or report lost & found pets.
  • NGOs and shelters managing cases and verifying reports.
  • Veterinary doctors and behaviour experts offering guidance.
  • Volunteers and animal lovers who help on the ground.

Together, we are building a safer, kinder network for animals across cities and communities.
