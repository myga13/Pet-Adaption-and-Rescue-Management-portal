Help & Awareness

Learn how PawCare, NGOs, and the community work together to keep animals safe and families informed.

Why NGO Verification?
Prevents fake profiles, supports honest adoptions, and protects pets from trafficking.
Safe Handover
Use written handover notes, ID verification and, if possible, NGO oversight.
Reporting Misuse
If anyone is asking for unusual payments or acting suspiciously, report inside the portal.
  • Always be transparent about your pet’s health and behaviour.
  • Never abandon an animal in public spaces; use shelters and NGOs.
  • Keep records of vaccines, vet visits and ID marks – this helps NGOs verify faster.
  • Teach family members (especially kids) how to approach new animals gently.
Awareness: Your actions on PawCare directly affect real animals and real families. Using NGO verification and honest reporting builds a safer ecosystem for everyone.
